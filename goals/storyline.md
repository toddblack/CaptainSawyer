# Captain Sawyer — Storyline & Crew

> Narrative source of truth — **canonical premise ✅ (confirmed 2026-09-28)**.
> Mark open questions with ❓ and resolved ones with ✅.
> Story drives the roadmap: map expansion → more exploration → finding crew and
> crashed capsules → salvaged tech → better boats → further out.

---

## Premise

A crewed deep-space exploration mission was en route (crew in hyper sleep) when their craft lost control approaching an unknown habitable world. On entry the craft broke apart. Crew escaped in paired abort capsules. They are now stranded on an alien ocean world with no comms, no charts, no idea if they are alone.

Captain Sawyer is female. Leader, innovator, supporter, cheerleader, guiding light.

**Tone:** Lonely at first → adventurous. Exploratory/survival. The question "are there inhabitants?" is left unanswered for a long time — environmental hints only (formations, worked-looking resources, a light on a distant shore at night).

## Crew Structure — 13 total, 7 capsules

All crew are cross-trained in basic survival. Each has a specialty focus.
Capsules carry 2 crew each (except C-0, Command). Pairs are designed to complement for maximum survival.
**No crew are lost — but not all are healthy or uninjured at discovery.**

| Capsule | Crew | Role | Start Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| C-0 (Command) | **Captain Sawyer** | Captain — solo capsule | Capsule sank. Sawyer washed ashore on starting island. |
| C-1 (Support) | **Biologist** | Studies alien flora/fauna — "don't touch anything" | Lands near Sawyer. Starting companions. |
| C-1 (Support) | **Surgeon** | Keeps everyone alive | Lands near Sawyer. Starting companions. |
| C-2 | **Structural Engineer** | Build shelter, then vessels | Scattered — findable mid-early game |
| C-2 | **Mechanical Engineer** | Machinery, propulsion | Scattered — findable mid-early game |
| C-3 | **Geologist** | Understand alien materials | Scattered |
| C-3 | **Chemist** | Make alien materials useful | Scattered |
| C-4 | **Navigator / Astronomer** | Read the sky | Scattered |
| C-4 | **Meteorologist** | Read the sea and weather | Scattered |
| C-5 | **Ecologist** | Alien ecosystem study | Scattered |
| C-5 | **Agriculturalist** | Can we grow food here? | Scattered |
| C-6 | **Pilot** | Vessel expertise | Deep unknown zone — late game |
| C-6 | **Tactician** | If things get complicated (inhabitants?) | Deep unknown zone — late game |

The number 13 is deliberate — the "unlucky" number fits a crew that crash-landed on an alien world.

## World

- Alien ocean planet — multi-biome islands fit perfectly as alien terrain
- No charts exist. Fog of war is literal: no satellites, no GPS, only eyes
- Starting island: where Sawyer and C-1 came down ("Sawyer's Rest" in `island_spawner.gd`)
- Sawyer's sunken capsule is in the shallows — potentially recoverable with later tech (diving)
- The current 1000×1000 map is the **starting zone**. More ocean unlocks as the player
  progresses; deeper/boundary zones hold stranger, higher-value islands and resources
